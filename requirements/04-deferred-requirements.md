# Deferred Requirements

These requirements were identified from the scenario but are not in the first-release catalogue, which is limited to 12 functional requirements. They are kept here for traceability and future releases.

## Deferred functional requirements

| ID | Original | Requirement | Priority | Reason deferred | Related |
|-------|----------|-------------|----------|-----------------|---------|
| DFR01 | FR02 | The system shall record how the request arrived (telephone, referral email or local support organisation) and the details of the referrer. | Should | Useful for referrer contact and reporting, but not needed to complete a journey. | – |
| DFR02 | FR04 (part) | The system shall allow a request to be saved as incomplete and show which details are missing. | Should | The blocking rule is kept as [FR02](01-functional-requirements.md#FR02); saving drafts is a usability improvement. | [A01](../report/05-assumptions.md#A01) |
| DFR03 | FR06 | The system shall store passenger records so that details for regular passengers can be reused for new requests. | Should | Reduces re-entry but journeys can be booked without it. | – |
| DFR04 | FR07 | The system shall record the outcome of an eligibility discussion for a new passenger, including the date, the staff member and the basis for the decision. | Should | Eligibility evidence is unresolved. | [Q01](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q01) |
| DFR05 | FR08 | The system shall flag passengers whose eligibility is due for review. | Could | Review period is unresolved. | [Q01](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q01) |
| DFR06 | FR10 | The system shall not provide fields for clinical diagnoses and shall allow staff to remove clinical information that a referrer includes by mistake. | Must | Strong candidate for the next release. Driver access is restricted by [NFR01](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01) in the meantime. | – |
| DFR07 | FR11 | The system shall store driver records, including driver type, general availability, preferred areas, whether they drive after dark and accessible-vehicle authorisation. | Must | Supporting data for [FR06](01-functional-requirements.md#FR06) and [FR12](01-functional-requirements.md#FR12); implied by those requirements. | [A04](../report/05-assumptions.md#A04) |
| DFR08 | FR12 | The system shall store vehicle records, including passenger capacity, wheelchair space and companion seating. | Must | Supporting data for [FR05](01-functional-requirements.md#FR05); implied by it. | – |
| DFR09 | FR13 | The system shall allow staff to record that a driver or vehicle is unavailable for a period. | Must | Supporting function for [FR12](01-functional-requirements.md#FR12); implied by it. | [A07](../report/05-assumptions.md#A07) |
| DFR10 | FR14 | The system shall list drivers and vehicles suitable for a journey, based on availability, area and after-dark preferences, passenger needs and vehicle capacity. | Should | Supports staff decisions; allocation can still be made manually. | – |
| DFR11 | FR15 (part) | The system shall prevent an accessible vehicle from being assigned to two journeys at the same time. | Must | Depends on whether provisional assignments reserve vehicles. Authorisation rule kept as [FR06](01-functional-requirements.md#FR06). | [Q03](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q03) |
| DFR12 | FR17 | The system shall allow passengers to be grouped into a shared journey only when each has agreed to share and their times, locations and vehicle capacity are compatible. | Could | Efficiency gain, not essential for coordinating journeys. | – |
| DFR13 | FR19 | The system shall allow a journey to be marked as provisional while waiting for the passenger, referrer or driver to confirm. | Should | Whether a provisional assignment reserves a driver or vehicle is unresolved. | [Q03](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q03) |
| DFR14 | FR22 | The system shall notify the referrer when a journey is confirmed. | Should | Notification method not stated. | – |
| DFR15 | FR23 | The system shall produce a driver journey sheet, printed and on a mobile device, showing directions, collection address, practical assistance needs and the coordinator's contact details, without unrelated personal details. | Must | Strong candidate for the next release. Partly supported by [FR10](01-functional-requirements.md#FR10) and [NFR03](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR03). | [A03](../report/05-assumptions.md#A03), [A08](../report/05-assumptions.md#A08) |
| DFR16 | FR25 | The system shall allow drivers to record exceptions (passenger not ready, not at the address, decided not to travel), the action taken and how long they waited. | Should | Maximum waiting time is unresolved. | [Q05](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q05) |
| DFR17 | FR26 | The system shall recheck whether the current driver and vehicle are still suitable when the appointment time, collection address, companion or finishing time changes. | Must | Changes are recorded by [FR03](01-functional-requirements.md#FR03); automatic rechecking depends on [DFR10](#DFR10). | – |
| DFR18 | FR28 (part) | The system shall support finding a replacement driver, sharing passenger details with them only once they have accepted the journey. | Must | Affected journeys are listed by [FR12](01-functional-requirements.md#FR12); disclosure is controlled by [NFR01](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01). | [A03](../report/05-assumptions.md#A03) |
| DFR19 | FR29 | The system shall record when transport cannot be provided and track that the passenger and referrer have been informed, including unsuccessful contact attempts. | Must | Notification order is unresolved. | [Q07](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q07) |
| DFR20 | FR30 | The system shall give the coordinator a view of unallocated, provisional and confirmed journeys, accessible-vehicle demand, driver availability, recent changes and incomplete records. | Should | Underlying data is captured by the main requirements; the view can follow. | – |
| DFR21 | FR31 | The system shall produce summary reports for funders that do not identify individual passengers. | Should | Not needed to coordinate journeys; report content not specified. | – |
| DFR22 | FR32 | The system shall provide separate roles for office staff, coordinator, volunteer drivers and employed drivers, each showing only the information that role requires. | Must | Driver restriction kept as [NFR01](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01); full role model deferred. | – |

## Deferred non-functional requirements

| ID | Original | Requirement | Reason deferred | Related |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| DNFR01 | NFR02 | The system shall not store clinical diagnoses or medical history. | Duplicates [DFR06](#DFR06). | – |
| DNFR02 | NFR03 | Funder reports shall contain only anonymised data. | Funder reporting deferred ([DFR21](#DFR21)). | – |
| DNFR03 | NFR04 | All users shall log in with an individual account, and access shall be controlled by role. | Partly covered by [NFR01](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01); full role model deferred ([DFR22](#DFR22)). | – |
| DNFR04 | NFR05 | The system shall keep an audit log of who viewed or changed passenger records. | Change history is covered by [FR03](01-functional-requirements.md#FR03); a view log is not required by the scenario. | – |
| DNFR05 | NFR06 | The system shall comply with GDPR, including data retention and passengers' right of access. | Not measurable until retention periods are confirmed. | [A17](../report/05-assumptions.md#A17), [Q20](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q20) |
| DNFR06 | NFR08 | Driver journey sheets shall be printable and readable without needing a device. | Depends on [DFR15](#DFR15). | [A08](../report/05-assumptions.md#A08), [Q14](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q14) |
| DNFR07 | NFR10 | The system shall prevent the same driver or accessible vehicle from being double booked, including when two staff members edit at the same time. | Depends on [DFR11](#DFR11) and [Q03](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q03). | [Q03](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q03) |
| DNFR08 | NFR11 | Unagreed policy values (eligibility review period, maximum waiting time, provisional reservation) shall be configurable without changing the software. | The features they configure are deferred. | [Q01](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q01), [Q03](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q03), [Q05](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q05) |
| DNFR09 | NFR12 | The system shall support growth in requests, drivers and passengers without a drop in performance. | Not measurable as written; the load is covered by [NFR02](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR02). | [A11](../report/05-assumptions.md#A11), [Q09](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q09) |
| DNFR10 | NFR16 | The system shall work on desktop browsers for office use and on mobile phones for drivers. | Platform constraint, implied by [FR10](01-functional-requirements.md#FR10) and [NFR03](02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR03). | [A08](../report/05-assumptions.md#A08) |
