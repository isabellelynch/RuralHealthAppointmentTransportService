# Functional Requirements

"A functional requirement describes an observable service, capability, calculation, validation or response that the proposed system must provide. A strong functional requirement is atomic, clear, solution-neutral and verifiable." ([Shields, 2026](../references/references.md#lectures))


| ID | Requirement | Priority | Source / Rationale |
|------|-------------|----------|--------------------|
| FR01 | The system shall record a transport request with the passenger, appointment location, appointment date and time, estimated duration or finishing time, collection address and travel needs. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: lists what a request normally includes. Core data for every journey. |
| FR02 | The system shall record how the request arrived (telephone, referral email or local support organization) and the details of the referrer. | Should | *Organisation and current service*: requests arrive by telephone, email or support organisations. Referrer details are needed to inform them later (FR22, FR29). |
| FR03 | The system shall record a contact person for each request, who may be different from the passenger (e.g. a family member). | Should | *Transport requests and eligibility*: referrers sometimes use a family member as the contact. |
| FR04 | The system shall allow a request to be saved as incomplete, show which details are missing (e.g. finishing time) and prevent it from being allocated until the required details are complete. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: finishing time is often missing and several calls may be needed. Coordinator: "the finishing time may be little more than a guess." |
| FR05 | The system shall allow a request to be updated after it is submitted and keep a history of what changed, when and who changed it. | Must | Referrer: "A request can change after I send it, especially when the clinic changes the appointment." |
| FR06 | The system shall store passenger records so that details for regular passengers can be reused for new requests. | Should | *Transport requests and eligibility*: regular passengers are familiar to staff. Reduces re-entry as demand grows. |
| FR07 | The system shall record the outcome of an eligibility discussion for a new passenger, including the date, the staff member and the basis for the decision. | Should | *Transport requests and eligibility*: new passengers may need an eligibility discussion. Staff want more reliable support for eligibility. |
| FR08 | The system shall flag passengers whose eligibility is due for review. The review period is not yet agreed (TBC). | Could | *Unresolved matters*: review period and evidence not confirmed. Can't be fully specified until policy is agreed. |
| FR09 | The system shall record practical travel needs: wheelchair space, assistance entering the vehicle, room for a walking aid, a companion travelling and whether the passenger agrees to share a journey. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: lists practical needs. Accessible-vehicle driver: "Wheelchair space and companion seating must be known before the vehicle is assigned." |
| FR10 | The system shall not provide fields for clinical diagnoses and shall allow staff to remove clinical information that a referrer includes by mistake. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: diagnoses not required but sometimes included. Volunteer driver: "I do not need a medical history." Clinical decision-making is out of scope. |
| FR11 | The system shall store driver records, including driver type (volunteer or employed), general availability, preferred areas, whether they drive after dark and whether they are authorized to drive an accessible vehicle. | Must | *Planning and driver allocation*: volunteers give general availability, prefer familiar areas, may not drive after dark. Accessible vehicles need authorised drivers. |
| FR12 | The system shall store vehicle records, including passenger capacity, wheelchair space and companion seating. | Must | *Planning and driver allocation*: vehicle capacity must be considered. Accessible-vehicle driver quote. |
| FR13 | The system shall allow staff to record that a driver or vehicle is unavailable for a period (e.g. illness, vehicle problems, severe weather). | Must | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: drivers may become unavailable due to illness, vehicle problems or severe weather. |
| FR14 | The system shall list drivers and vehicles suitable for a journey, based on availability, area and after-dark preferences, passenger needs and vehicle capacity. | Must | *Planning and driver allocation*: staff try to allocate a suitable driver and vehicle. Supports staff decisions but allocation can still be done manually. |
| FR15 | The system shall only allow an accessible vehicle to be assigned to an authorized driver, and shall prevent it from being assigned to two journeys at the same time. | Must | *Planning and driver allocation*: accessible vehicles require authorised drivers and may already be committed to another passenger. |
| FR16 | The system shall prevent a vehicle from being assigned to a journey if it cannot meet the passenger's wheelchair or companion seating needs. | Must | Accessible-vehicle driver: "Wheelchair space and companion seating must be known before the vehicle is assigned." Passenger safety. |
| FR17 | The system shall allow passengers to be grouped into a shared journey only when each passenger has agreed to share and their appointment times, locations and the vehicle capacity are compatible. | Could | *Planning and driver allocation*: shared journeys "can be efficient". An efficiency gain, not essential for the first release. |
| FR18 | The system shall record every attempt to contact a driver about a journey and its outcome (accepted, declined, no answer). | Should | Office staff: "the spreadsheet only shows the person who finally accepted." Unsuccessful contacts not recorded consistently. |
| FR19 | The system shall allow a journey to be marked as provisional while waiting for the passenger, referrer or driver to confirm. Whether a provisional assignment reserves the driver or vehicle is not yet agreed (TBC). | Should | *Planning and driver allocation*: journeys may be provisionally assigned. *Unresolved matters*: staff disagree on reservation. |
| FR20 | The system shall record the outward and return stages of a journey separately, each with its own driver, status and timing, so that a different driver can complete the return. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: not recording stages separately "can cause confusion when plans change". *Unresolved matters*: representation of stages. |
| FR21 | The system shall produce a passenger confirmation showing the collection window, the driver or service contact, whether the driver will wait or return later and any contribution towards travel costs. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: lists confirmation contents. Passenger: "Please tell me whether the driver is waiting or coming back later." |
| FR22 | The system shall notify the referrer when a journey is confirmed. The method of notification is not stated (A). | Should | *Information and organisational context*: referring organisations need information. Notification on confirmation is an assumption (A). |
| FR23 | The system shall produce a driver journey sheet, available printed and on a mobile device, showing directions, collection address, practical assistance needs and the coordinator's contact details, without unrelated personal details. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: drivers need directions and assistance info, not unrelated personal details. *Information and organisational context*: uneven mobile coverage, some prefer printed sheets. |
| FR24 | The system shall allow drivers to record collection, arrival and return completion for each stage of a journey. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: "The driver records collection, arrival and return completion." |
| FR25 | The system shall allow drivers to record exceptions (passenger not ready, not at the agreed address, decided not to travel), what action was taken (waited, contacted the coordinator, left) and how long they waited. | Should | *Journey confirmation and completion*: lists exceptions and driver actions. *Unresolved matters*: no agreed maximum waiting time. |
| FR26 | The system shall allow changes to appointment time, collection address, companion and expected finishing time and shall recheck whether the current driver and vehicle are still suitable. | Must | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: times change at short notice, passengers may need a companion or a different address, or be kept longer. |
| FR27 | The system shall record each cancellation with who cancelled, a reason why, how much notice was given and whether the change came from the healthcare provider. | Should | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: cancellation reasons recorded inconsistently. Passengers shouldn't be disadvantaged by provider changes. |
| FR28 | When a driver becomes unavailable, the system shall flag their affected journeys and support finding a replacement, sharing passenger details with a replacement driver only once they have accepted the journey (A). | Must | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: staff find replacements "without disclosing passenger information more widely than necessary". Timing of disclosure is an assumption (A). |
| FR29 | The system shall record when transport cannot be provided and track that the passenger and referrer have been informed, including unsuccessful contact attempts. Who should be contacted first is not yet agreed (TBC). | Must | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: passenger and referrer "should be informed promptly". *Unresolved matters*: who to notify first. |
| FR30 | The system shall give the coordinator a view of unallocated requests, provisional journeys, confirmed journeys, accessible vehicle demand, driver availability, recent changes and incomplete journey records. | Must | *Information and organisational context*: coordinator needs reliable views of these items. |
| FR31 | The system shall produce summary reports for funders (e.g. journeys completed, cancellations by reason, accessible vehicle use) that do not identify individual passengers. Report content is not specified (A). | Should | *Information and organisational context*: summary reports required for funders, without identifying passengers unnecessarily. Not needed to coordinate journeys day to day. |
| FR32 | The system shall provide separate roles for office staff, coordinator, volunteer drivers and employed drivers. | Must | *Information and organisational context*: different users need different information. Staff want support for confidential information. |
