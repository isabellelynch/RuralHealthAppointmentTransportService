# Functional Requirements

"A functional requirement describes an observable service, capability, calculation, validation or response that the proposed system must provide. A strong functional requirement is atomic, clear, solution-neutral and verifiable." ([Shields, 2026](../references/01-text-sources.md#lectures))


| ID | Requirement | Priority | Source / Rationale | Assumptions / Questions | Verification |
|------|-------------|----------|--------------------|-------------------------|--------------|
| <a id="FR01"></a>FR01 | The system shall record a transport request with the passenger, appointment location, appointment date and time, estimated finishing time, collection address and contact person. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: lists what a request normally includes; referrers may use a family member as contact. | [A01](../report/05-assumptions.md#A01), [Q08](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q08) | Test: create a request with all fields; each is saved and displayed. |
| <a id="FR02"></a>FR02 | The system shall prevent a request from being allocated to a driver while any mandatory field is empty. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: finishing time often missing. Coordinator: "the finishing time may be little more than a guess." | [A01](../report/05-assumptions.md#A01), [A02](../report/05-assumptions.md#A02), [Q08](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q08) | Test: allocate a request with no finishing time; allocation is blocked and the missing field is named. |
| <a id="FR03"></a>FR03 | The system shall record a history of changes to a request, showing the old value, new value, date and user. | Must | Referrer: "A request can change after I send it, especially when the clinic changes the appointment." | – | Test: change the appointment time; history shows old and new time, date and user. |
| <a id="FR04"></a>FR04 | The system shall record a passenger's practical travel needs: wheelchair space, boarding assistance, walking aid, companion and consent to share a journey. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: lists practical needs. | [A03](../report/05-assumptions.md#A03), [Q02](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q02) | Test: record each need for a passenger; each is saved and shown on the request. |
| <a id="FR05"></a>FR05 | The system shall prevent a vehicle from being assigned to a journey if its wheelchair spaces or companion seats are fewer than the passenger requires. | Must | Accessible-vehicle driver: "Wheelchair space and companion seating must be known before the vehicle is assigned." | – | Test: assign a car with no wheelchair space to a wheelchair user; assignment is rejected. |
| <a id="FR06"></a>FR06 | The system shall prevent an accessible vehicle from being assigned to a driver who is not authorised to drive it. | Must | *Planning and driver allocation*: "Accessible vehicles require authorised drivers." | [A04](../report/05-assumptions.md#A04), [Q18](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q18) | Test: assign an accessible vehicle to an unauthorised volunteer; assignment is rejected. |
| <a id="FR07"></a>FR07 | The system shall record each attempt to contact a driver about a journey with its date, time and outcome (accepted, declined, no answer). | Should | Office staff: "the spreadsheet only shows the person who finally accepted." | [A09](../report/05-assumptions.md#A09), [Q17](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q17) | Test: log three attempts with different outcomes; all three are listed against the journey. |
| <a id="FR08"></a>FR08 | The system shall record the outward and return stages of a journey separately, each with its own driver and status. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: not recording stages separately "can cause confusion when plans change". | [A05](../report/05-assumptions.md#A05), [Q04](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q04) | Test: assign different drivers to outward and return; each stage shows its own driver and status. |
| <a id="FR09"></a>FR09 | The system shall produce a passenger confirmation stating the collection window, the contact number and whether the driver will wait or return later. | Must | Passenger: "Please tell me whether the driver is waiting or coming back later." | [A10](../report/05-assumptions.md#A10), [Q15](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q15) | Inspection: confirmation for a sample journey contains all three items. |
| <a id="FR10"></a>FR10 | The system shall allow a driver to record the time of collection, arrival and return completion for each journey stage. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: "The driver records collection, arrival and return completion." | [A08](../report/05-assumptions.md#A08), [Q14](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q14) | Demonstration: driver records each status on a mobile device; times appear in the coordinator view. |
| <a id="FR11"></a>FR11 | The system shall record each cancellation with who cancelled, the reason, the notice given and whether the healthcare provider caused it. | Should | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: reasons recorded inconsistently; passengers shouldn't be disadvantaged by provider changes. | [A06](../report/05-assumptions.md#A06), [Q06](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q06) | Test: cancel a journey as provider-initiated; record shows all four values and can be filtered by reason. |
| <a id="FR12"></a>FR12 | The system shall list all journeys affected when a driver is marked unavailable for a period. | Must | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: drivers may become unavailable through illness, vehicle problems or severe weather. | [A07](../report/05-assumptions.md#A07), [Q16](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q16) | Test: mark a driver unavailable for 2 days; every journey assigned to them in that period is listed. |
