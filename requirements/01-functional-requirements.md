# Functional Requirements

"A functional requirement describes an observable service, capability, calculation, validation or response that the proposed system must provide. A strong functional requirement is atomic, clear, solution-neutral and verifiable." ([Shields, 2026](../references/01-text-sources.md#lectures))

The requirements below cover the core coordination workflow for the first release: capturing requests, checking allocations, recording journey progress and changes, protecting passenger information and reporting to funders. Each is a single "shall" statement with a stated verification method. Priorities use MoSCoW ([Clegg and Barker, 1994](../references/01-text-sources.md#clegg)). Further candidates found in the scenario are held in [deferred requirements](03-deferred-requirements.md).

**Prioritisation.** Each priority was assigned by the tests below.

| Priority | Test applied | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Must | Without it, the first release fails a [desired outcome](../report/01-scenario-interpretation.md#desired-outcomes), a business rule or a legal constraint. | FR05 and FR06 enforce [BR05](../report/06-rules-and-uncertainty-register.md#BR05) and [BR04](../report/06-rules-and-uncertainty-register.md#BR04), FR13 and FR14 meet funder and data protection obligations. |
| Should | Clear value, but staff could work around its absence for a short time. | FR07 and FR11 improve today's inconsistent records, but journeys can still be coordinated without them. |
| Could | A convenience or efficiency gain. | Deferred, for example shared journeys ([DFR08](03-deferred-requirements.md#DFR08)). |
| Won't (this release) | Depends on an unresolved policy or is outside the service. | [Deferred requirements](03-deferred-requirements.md) and [out-of-scope items](../report/02-purpose-and-scope.md#out-of-scope). |

There are fourteen functional requirements, slightly above the eight to twelve suggested, because funder reporting and clinical-information removal were brought forward to meet explicit obligations ([D02](../decisions/00-decision-log.md#D02)).

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Source / rationale | Assumptions / questions | Verification |
|------|-------------|----------|--------------------|-------------------------|--------------|
| <a id="FR01"></a>FR01 | The system shall record a transport request with the passenger, appointment location, appointment date and time, estimated finishing time, collection address and contact person. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: lists what a request normally includes. Referrers may use a family member as contact. | [A01](../report/05-assumptions.md#A01), [Q08](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q08) | Test: create a request with all fields, each is saved and displayed. |
| <a id="FR02"></a>FR02 | The system shall prevent a request from being allocated to a driver while any mandatory field is empty. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: finishing times are often missing. Coordinator: "the finishing time may be little more than a guess." | [A01](../report/05-assumptions.md#A01), [A02](../report/05-assumptions.md#A02), [Q08](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q08) | Test: try to allocate a request with no finishing time, allocation is blocked and the missing field is named. |
| <a id="FR03"></a>FR03 | The system shall record a history of changes to a request, showing the old value, new value, date and user. | Must | Referrer: "A request can change after I send it, especially when the clinic changes the appointment." |  | Test: change the appointment time, the history shows the old and new time, the date and the user. |
| <a id="FR04"></a>FR04 | The system shall record a passenger's practical travel needs: wheelchair space, boarding assistance, walking aid, companion and agreement to share a journey. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: lists practical needs. | [A03](../report/05-assumptions.md#A03), [Q02](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q02) | Test: record each need for a passenger, each is saved and shown on the request. |
| <a id="FR05"></a>FR05 | The system shall prevent a vehicle from being assigned to a journey if its wheelchair spaces or companion seats are fewer than the passenger requires. | Must | Accessible-vehicle driver: "Wheelchair space and companion seating must be known before the vehicle is assigned." |  | Test: assign a car with no wheelchair space to a wheelchair user, the assignment is rejected. |
| <a id="FR06"></a>FR06 | The system shall prevent an accessible vehicle from being assigned to a driver who is not authorised to drive it. | Must | *Planning and driver allocation*: "Accessible vehicles require authorised drivers." | [A04](../report/05-assumptions.md#A04), [Q18](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q18) | Test: assign an accessible vehicle to an unauthorised volunteer, the assignment is rejected. |
| <a id="FR07"></a>FR07 | The system shall record each attempt to contact a driver about a journey with its date, time and outcome (accepted, declined, no answer). | Should | Office staff: "the spreadsheet only shows the person who finally accepted." | [A09](../report/05-assumptions.md#A09), [Q17](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q17) | Test: log three attempts with different outcomes, all three are listed against the journey. |
| <a id="FR08"></a>FR08 | The system shall record the outward and return stages of a journey separately, each with its own driver and status. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: not recording stages separately "can cause confusion when plans change". | [A05](../report/05-assumptions.md#A05), [Q04](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q04) | Test: assign different drivers to the outward and return stages, each stage shows its own driver and status. |
| <a id="FR09"></a>FR09 | The system shall produce a passenger confirmation stating the collection window, the driver or service contact number, whether the driver will wait or return later and any contribution towards travel costs. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: passengers should receive "the collection window, driver or service contact, expected arrangements and any contribution towards travel costs". Passenger: "Please tell me whether the driver is waiting or coming back later." | [A10](../report/05-assumptions.md#A10), [Q15](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q15) | Inspection: the confirmation for a sample journey contains all four items. |
| <a id="FR10"></a>FR10 | The system shall allow a driver to record the time of collection, arrival and return completion for each journey stage. | Must | *Journey confirmation and completion*: "The driver records collection, arrival and return completion." | [A08](../report/05-assumptions.md#A08), [Q14](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q14) | Demonstration: a driver records each status on a mobile device, the times appear in the coordinator's view. |
| <a id="FR11"></a>FR11 | The system shall record each cancellation with who cancelled, the reason, the notice given and whether the healthcare provider caused it. | Should | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: reasons are recorded inconsistently and passengers should not be disadvantaged by provider changes. | [A06](../report/05-assumptions.md#A06), [Q06](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q06) | Test: cancel a journey as provider-initiated, the record shows all four values and can be filtered by reason. |
| <a id="FR12"></a>FR12 | The system shall list all journeys affected when a driver is marked unavailable for a period. | Must | *Changes, cancellations and disruption*: drivers may become unavailable through illness, vehicle problems or severe weather. | [A07](../report/05-assumptions.md#A07), [Q16](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q16) | Test: mark a driver unavailable for two days, every journey assigned to them in that period is listed. |
| <a id="FR13"></a>FR13 | The system shall produce a summary report for a chosen date range showing the number of requests, completed journeys, cancellations by reason and accessible-vehicle journeys, without passenger names, addresses or contact details. | Must | *Information and organisational context*: "Summary reports are required for funders, but should not identify passengers unnecessarily." | [A18](../report/05-assumptions.md#A18), [Q21](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q21) | Test: generate a report for one month of test data, totals match the journey records, no identifying detail appears. |
| <a id="FR14"></a>FR14 | The system shall allow office staff to permanently remove clinical information from a request's notes, including from its change history. | Must | *Transport requests and eligibility*: staff do not require diagnoses, but referrers sometimes include them. Volunteer driver: "I do not need a medical history." | [A19](../report/05-assumptions.md#A19), [Q22](../report/04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q22) | Test: enter a diagnosis in a request's notes, then remove it, the text no longer appears in the request, its change history or the driver's view. |

**Supporting data.** FR05, FR06 and FR12 depend on driver records (type, availability, preferences and accessible-vehicle authorisation), vehicle records (capacity, wheelchair spaces and companion seats) and recorded periods of unavailability. These are treated as data that those requirements need, shown in the [domain model](../report/10-domain-model.md), rather than as separate requirements.
