# Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| FR01 | The system shall record a transport request with the passenger, appointment location, appointment date and time, estimated duration or finishing time, collection address and travel needs. |
| FR02 | The system shall record how the request arrived (telephone, referral email or local support organization) and the details of the referrer. |
| FR03 | The system shall record a contact person for each request, who may be different from the passenger (e.g. a family member). |
| FR04 | The system shall allow a request to be saved as incomplete, show which details are missing (e.g. finishing time) and prevent it from being allocated until the required details are complete. |
| FR05 | The system shall allow a request to be updated after it is submitted and keep a history of what changed, when and who changed it. |
| FR06 | The system shall store passenger records so that details for regular passengers can be reused for new requests. |
| FR07 | The system shall record the outcome of an eligibility discussion for a new passenger, including the date, the staff member and the basis for the decision. |
| FR08 | The system shall flag passengers whose eligibility is due for review. The review period is not yet agreed (TBC). |
| FR09 | The system shall record practical travel needs: wheelchair space, assistance entering the vehicle, room for a walking aid, a companion travelling and whether the passenger agrees to share a journey. |
| FR10 | The system shall not provide fields for clinical diagnoses and shall allow staff to remove clinical information that a referrer includes by mistake. |
| FR11 | The system shall store driver records, including driver type (volunteer or employed), general availability, preferred areas, whether they drive after dark and whether they are authorized to drive an accessible vehicle. |
| FR12 | The system shall store vehicle records, including passenger capacity, wheelchair space and companion seating. |
| FR13 | The system shall allow staff to record that a driver or vehicle is unavailable for a period (e.g. illness, vehicle problems, severe weather). |
| FR14 | The system shall list drivers and vehicles suitable for a journey, based on availability, area and after-dark preferences, passenger needs and vehicle capacity. |
| FR15 | The system shall only allow an accessible vehicle to be assigned to an authorized driver, and shall prevent it from being assigned to two journeys at the same time. |
| FR16 | The system shall prevent a vehicle from being assigned to a journey if it cannot meet the passenger's wheelchair or companion seating needs. |
| FR17 | The system shall allow passengers to be grouped into a shared journey only when each passenger has agreed to share and their appointment times, locations and the vehicle capacity are compatible. |
| FR18 | The system shall record every attempt to contact a driver about a journey and its outcome (accepted, declined, no answer). |
| FR19 | The system shall allow a journey to be marked as provisional while waiting for the passenger, referrer or driver to confirm. Whether a provisional assignment reserves the driver or vehicle is not yet agreed (TBC). |
| FR20 | The system shall record the outward and return stages of a journey separately, each with its own driver, status and timing, so that a different driver can complete the return. |
| FR21 | The system shall produce a passenger confirmation showing the collection window, the driver or service contact, whether the driver will wait or return later and any contribution towards travel costs. |
| FR22 | The system shall notify the referrer when a journey is confirmed. The method of notification is not stated (A). |
| FR23 | The system shall produce a driver journey sheet, available printed and on a mobile device, showing directions, collection address, practical assistance needs and the coordinator's contact details, without unrelated personal details. |
| FR24 | The system shall allow drivers to record collection, arrival and return completion for each stage of a journey. |
| FR25 | The system shall allow drivers to record exceptions (passenger not ready, not at the agreed address, decided not to travel), what action was taken (waited, contacted the coordinator, left) and how long they waited. |
| FR26 | The system shall allow changes to appointment time, collection address, companion and expected finishing time and shall recheck whether the current driver and vehicle are still suitable. |
| FR27 | The system shall record each cancellation with who cancelled, a reason why, how much notice was given and whether the change came from the healthcare provider. |
| FR28 | When a driver becomes unavailable, the system shall flag their affected journeys and support finding a replacement, sharing passenger details with a replacement driver only once they have accepted the journey (A). |
| FR29 | The system shall record when transport cannot be provided and track that the passenger and referrer have been informed, including unsuccessful contact attempts. Who should be contacted first is not yet agreed (TBC). |
| FR30 | The system shall give the coordinator a view of unallocated requests, provisional journeys, confirmed journeys, accessible vehicle demand, driver availability, recent changes and incomplete journey records. |
| FR31 | The system shall produce summary reports for funders (e.g. journeys completed, cancellations by reason, accessible vehicle use) that do not identify individual passengers. Report content is not specified (A). |
| FR32 | The system shall provide separate roles for office staff, coordinator, volunteer drivers and employed drivers, each showing only the information that role requires. |
