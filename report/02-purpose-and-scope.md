# Purpose and Scope

## Purpose Statement

The system will support the network in coordinating planned, non-emergency transport to healthcare appointments, 
from receiving requests through to completing journeys. It will help staff record passenger needs, allocate 
suitable drivers and vehicles and manage changes, while sharing personal information only where necessary.

## In Scope
- Recording transport requests, including appointment details, collection address, contact person and travel needs.
- Recording each passenger's eligibility status.
- Recording practical travel needs such as wheelchair space, assistance, walking aids, companions and whether they will share a journey.
- Recording driver availability, perferences and restrictions.
- Allocating drivers and vehicles to journeys, including shared journeys.
- Recording a journey as provisional or confirmed.
- Recording outward and return journeys seperately.
- Recording driver contact attempts, including declined offers.
- Confirming arrangements with passengers: collection window, contact details and any travel contribution.
- Giving drivers only the information they need for a journey, available in print as well as on a phone.
- Recording collection, arrival, return completion and passengers who did not travel.
- Recording changes and cancellations, including the reason and who initiated them.
- Notifying the passenger and referrer when no transport can be provided.
- Giving the coordinator an overview of unallocated, provisional, confirmed and incomplete journeys.
- Producing summary reports for funders that do not identify passengers.

## Out of Scope
- Emergency transport or dispatch.
- Direct access to healthcare or clinical records.
- Clinical decision making.
- Booking or changing healthcare appointments with providers.
- Processing payments for travel contributions.
- Driver recruitment, training and vehicle maintenance.
- Live vehicle tracking or turn-by-turn navigation.
