# Stakeholder Questions

The scenario leaves several policies and details undecided, and the brief asks that these be raised rather than invented. Questions come from two sources: the scenario's own unresolved matters (Q01 to Q07), and evidence gaps behind the temporary assumptions in this report (Q08 to Q22). Each question names who should answer it and which requirements depend on the answer, so that answers can be fed back into the specification without rereading it ([Wiegers and Beatty, 2013](../references/01-text-sources.md#wiegers)).

*(A) = role assumed to exist. Not named in the scenario.*
