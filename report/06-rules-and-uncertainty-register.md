# Rules and Uncertainty Register

This register separates three things that are easily confused with requirements. **Business rules** are policies of the organisation that hold whether or not a system exists; requirements describe how the system supports them ([Wiegers and Beatty, 2013](../references/01-text-sources.md#wiegers)). **Constraints** limit the solution and are not negotiable or prioritised. **Risks** are uncertain events that could affect success, each with a mitigation ([IIBA, 2015](../references/01-text-sources.md#babok)). Recording them separately makes clear which parts of the specification are fixed and which depend on decisions still to be made.

| ID | Constraint | Type | Source | Impact |

## Risks

Risks are "an uncertain event or condition that may affect success" ([Shields, 2026](../references/01-text-sources.md#lectures)). Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are rated High (H), Medium (M) or Low (L).

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Related |
|------|------|:-:|:-:|------------|---------|
| <a id="R01"></a>R01 | Unresolved policies delay or change the design. | H | M | Raise questions early; defer dependent requirements; make policy values configurable when built. | [C09](#C09), [Q01](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q01), [Q03](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q03), [Q05](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q05), [Q07](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q07) |
| <a id="R02"></a>R02 | Passenger details are disclosed to a driver who has not accepted the journey. | L | H | Test role-based access before go-live; drivers see only accepted journeys. | [NFR01](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01), [BR07](#BR07) |
| <a id="R03"></a>R03 | Driver status updates are lost or delayed by poor mobile coverage. | H | M | Offline storage with automatic sending; printed sheet as fallback. | [NFR03](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR03), [C02](#C02) |
| <a id="R04"></a>R04 | Volunteer drivers do not adopt the mobile view (no smartphone, low confidence, preference for paper). | M | M | Confirm device use with drivers; keep a paper route; simple interface and short training. | [FR10](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR10), [A08](05-assumptions.md#A08), [Q14](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q14), [C03](#C03) |
| <a id="R05"></a>R05 | Assumed performance, availability and recovery targets are wrong, so the system is under- or over-built. | M | M | Confirm targets before design is finalised. | [A11](05-assumptions.md#A11)–[A15](05-assumptions.md#A15), [Q09](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q09)–[Q13](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q13) |
| <a id="R06"></a>R06 | Data migrated from the spreadsheet, calendar and paper sheets is incomplete or inaccurate. | M | H | Clean and check data before migration; run old and new processes in parallel for an agreed period. | [C08](#C08) |
| <a id="R07"></a>R07 | Referrers continue to include clinical information in requests. | H | M | No diagnosis field; staff remove clinical text; guidance to referring organisations. | [FR14](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR14), [C07](#C07) |
| <a id="R08"></a>R08 | Short-notice appointment changes leave confirmed journeys out of date. | H | M | Change history on every request; coordinator reviews recent changes. | [FR03](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR03), [DFR13](../requirements/03-deferred-requirements.md#DFR13) |
| <a id="R09"></a>R09 | With only two accessible vehicles, one becoming unavailable leaves wheelchair users without transport. | M | H | Flag affected journeys immediately; inform passenger and referrer promptly. | [FR12](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR12), [C04](#C04), [BR10](#BR10) |
| <a id="R10"></a>R10 | Limited budget and staff time reduce training and support, slowing adoption. | M | M | Prioritise Must requirements; phase the release; train key staff first. | [C10](#C10) |
| <a id="R11"></a>R11 | Deferred features (shared journeys, provisional reservations, coordinator overview) are pulled into the first release. | M | M | Keep the deferred list under change control; agree scope with the coordinator. | [Purpose and Scope](02-purpose-and-scope.md), [Deferred requirements](../requirements/03-deferred-requirements.md) |
| <a id="R12"></a>R12 | Removing clinical text conflicts with keeping a full change history, so the diagnosis survives as an "old value". | M | M | Removal also clears the text from the history and records only that a removal took place. | [FR03](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR03), [FR14](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR14) |
| <a id="R13"></a>R13 | Small counts in funder reports identify passengers in small rural communities. | M | H | Suppress counts below an agreed threshold. | [FR13](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR13), [A18](05-assumptions.md#A18), [BR11](#BR11) |
