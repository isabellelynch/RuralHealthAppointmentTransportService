# Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Reason / evidence gap | Affects | Risk if wrong | Confirm via |
|-----|------------|-----------------------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| A01 | The mandatory fields before allocation are passenger, appointment location, appointment date and time, estimated finishing time, collection address and contact person. | *Transport requests and eligibility* lists what a request "normally" includes but not which fields are required. | FR01, FR02 | Requests blocked unnecessarily, or allocated with key details missing. | Q08 |
| A02 | An estimated finishing time is enough to complete a request; an exact time is not required. | Coordinator: "the finishing time may be little more than a guess." | FR02 | If exact times were required, most requests could never be allocated. | Q08 |
| A03 | Drivers may see collection address, destination, contact number and practical needs (wheelchair, boarding assistance, walking aid, companion) only. | *Unresolved matters*: which needs may be shared with a driver is not confirmed. | FR04, NFR01 | Drivers lack information to help safely, or see more than necessary. | Q02 |
| A04 | Accessible-vehicle authorisation is a yes/no setting per driver, maintained by office staff. | Scenario says authorisation is required but not how it is granted or whether it expires. | FR06 | Expired or partial authorisations not enforced. | Q18 |
| A05 | Outward and return are two linked stages of one journey, each with its own driver and status. | *Unresolved matters*: how stages are represented is not confirmed. | FR08 | Data model needs rework if journeys must be split differently. | Q04 |
| A06 | Whether a cancellation was caused by the healthcare provider is recorded from what the passenger or referrer reports; it is not verified. | *Unresolved matters*: how provider responsibility is recorded is not confirmed. | FR11 | Cancellation reports used unfairly against passengers. | Q06 |
| A07 | Office staff record driver unavailability; drivers do not enter it themselves. | Scenario does not say who records unavailability. | FR12 | Unavailability recorded late, so affected journeys are found late. | Q16 |
| A08 | Drivers have a smartphone with a web browser; drivers without one use printed journey sheets. | *Information and organisational context*: some volunteers prefer printed sheets. Device ownership not stated. | FR10, NFR03 | Status updates not recorded by some drivers. | Q14 |
| A09 | Staff contact drivers by phone and log the outcome manually; the system does not contact drivers itself. | Scenario describes telephoning drivers; no automated contact mentioned. | FR07 | Scope grows if automated SMS or calls are expected. | Q17 |
| A10 | Passengers are confirmed by phone, text or letter with a 30-minute collection window. | Scenario requires a "collection window" but gives no width or channel. | FR09 | Window too narrow to keep or too wide to be useful. | Q15 |
| A11 | Data volume is up to 60 drivers and 1,000 journeys per month. | *Organisation and current service*: "Demand has increased." No figures given. | NFR02 | Performance targets set against the wrong load. | Q09 |
| A12 | Saving a driver or vehicle assignment must complete within 3 seconds for 95% of attempts. | No response-time target stated; staff assign journeys while on the phone. | NFR02 | Target too strict (cost) or too loose (slow phone calls). | Q10 |
| A13 | Drivers may be without coverage for up to 8 hours; updates must sync within 60 seconds of reconnecting. | *Information and organisational context*: "Mobile coverage is uneven." No duration given. | NFR03 | Updates lost or delayed beyond what the coordinator can tolerate. | Q11 |
| A14 | The system must be available 99% of the time between 06:00 and 22:00, seven days a week. | Service hours not stated; journeys include early mornings and evenings. | NFR04 | Outages during journeys, or over-engineering for hours not used. | Q12 |
| A15 | Backups at least every 24 hours, restorable within 4 hours. | No recovery targets stated. | NFR05 | More booking data lost after a failure than the service can tolerate. | Q13 |
| A16 | WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the required accessibility standard. | No standard named in the scenario. | NFR06 | Funder or legal requirement for a different standard. | Q19 |
| A17 | GDPR applies, South Kerry Community Travel Network is the data controller, and retention periods are to be set by the organisation. | Scenario stresses confidentiality but does not mention data protection law or retention. | NFR01, NFR05 | Data kept too long or deleted too soon. | Q20 |
