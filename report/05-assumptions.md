# Assumptions

An assumption is something believed to be true for planning purposes but not yet confirmed ([IIBA, 2015](../references/01-text-sources.md#babok)). The scenario asks that uncertainty be identified rather than replaced with an invented policy, so every assumption below is temporary. Each one records the evidence gap behind it, the requirements it affects, the risk if it proves wrong, and the stakeholder question that would confirm or replace it. Assumptions that affect a Must requirement should be confirmed first.

| ID | Assumption | Reason / evidence gap | Affects | Risk if wrong | Confirm via |
|-----|------------|-----------------------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| <a id="A01"></a>A01 | The mandatory details before allocation are passenger, appointment location, appointment date and time, estimated finishing time, collection address and contact person. | *Transport requests and eligibility* lists what a request "normally" includes, not which details are required. | [FR01](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR01), [FR02](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR02) | Requests blocked unnecessarily, or allocated with key details missing. | [Q08](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q08) |
| <a id="A02"></a>A02 | An estimated finishing time is enough to complete a request; an exact time is not required. | Coordinator: "the finishing time may be little more than a guess." | [FR02](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR02) | If exact times were required, most requests could never be allocated. | [Q08](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q08) |
| <a id="A03"></a>A03 | Drivers may see the collection address, destination, contact number and practical needs (wheelchair, boarding assistance, walking aid, companion) only. | *Unresolved matters*: which needs may be shared with a driver is not confirmed. | [FR04](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR04), [NFR01](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01) | Drivers lack information to help safely, or see more than necessary. | [Q02](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q02) |
| <a id="A04"></a>A04 | Accessible-vehicle authorisation is a yes/no setting per driver, maintained by office staff. | The scenario says authorisation is required but not how it is granted or whether it expires. | [FR06](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR06) | Expired or partial authorisations are not enforced. | [Q18](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q18) |
| <a id="A05"></a>A05 | Outward and return are two linked stages of one journey, each with its own driver and status. | *Unresolved matters*: how stages are represented is not confirmed. | [FR08](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR08) | The data model needs rework if journeys must be split differently. | [Q04](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q04) |
| <a id="A06"></a>A06 | Whether a cancellation was caused by the healthcare provider is recorded from what the passenger or referrer reports; it is not verified. | *Unresolved matters*: how provider responsibility is recorded is not confirmed. | [FR11](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR11) | Cancellation records are used unfairly against passengers. | [Q06](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q06) |
| <a id="A07"></a>A07 | Office staff record driver unavailability; drivers do not enter it themselves. | The scenario does not say who records unavailability. | [FR12](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR12) | Unavailability is recorded late, so affected journeys are found late. | [Q16](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q16) |
| <a id="A08"></a>A08 | Most drivers have a smartphone with a web browser; drivers without one use printed journey sheets and phone in their updates. | *Information and organisational context*: some volunteers prefer printed sheets. | <a id="A09"></a>A09 | Staff contact drivers by phone and log the outcome; the system does not contact drivers itself. | The scenario describes staff telephoning drivers; automated contact is not mentioned. | [FR07](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR07) | Scope grows if automated texts or calls are expected. | [Q17](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q17) |
| <a id="A10"></a>A10 | Passengers are confirmed by phone, text or letter, with a 30-minute collection window. | The scenario requires a "collection window" but gives no width or channel. | [FR09](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR09) | The window is too narrow to keep or too wide to be useful. | [Q15](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q15) |
| <a id="A11"></a>A11 | Data volume is up to 60 drivers and 1,000 journeys per month. | *Organisation and current service*: "Demand has increased." No figures are given. | [NFR02](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR02) | Performance targets are set against the wrong load. | [Q09](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q09) |
| <a id="A12"></a>A12 | Saving a driver or vehicle assignment must complete within 3 seconds for 95% of attempts. | No response-time target is stated; staff assign journeys while on the phone. | [NFR02](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR02) | Target too strict (cost) or too loose (slow phone calls). | [Q10](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q10) |
| <a id="A13"></a>A13 | Drivers may be without coverage for up to 8 hours; updates must be sent within 60 seconds of reconnecting. | *Information and organisational context*: "Mobile coverage is uneven." No duration is given. | [NFR03](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR03) | Updates are lost or arrive later than the coordinator can tolerate. | [Q11](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q11) |
| <a id="A14"></a>A14 | The system must be available 99% of the time between 06:00 and 22:00, seven days a week. | Service hours are not stated; journeys include early mornings and evenings. | [NFR04](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR04) | Outages during journeys, or cost spent on hours that are not used. | [Q12](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q12) |
| <a id="A15"></a>A15 | Data is backed up at least every 24 hours and can be restored within 4 hours. | No recovery targets are stated. | [NFR05](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR05) | More booking data is lost after a failure than the service can tolerate. | [Q13](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q13) |
| <a id="A16"></a>A16 | WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the required accessibility standard ([W3C, 2023](../references/01-text-sources.md#wcag)). | No standard is named in the scenario; 2.2 is the current W3C recommendation. | [NFR06](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR06) | Funders or law require a different standard. | [Q19](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q19) |
| <a id="A17"></a>A17 | The GDPR and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018 apply, the network is the data controller, and retention periods are set by the network ([European Parliament and Council, 2016](../references/01-text-sources.md#gdpr); [Ireland, 2018](../references/01-text-sources.md#dpa2018)). | The scenario stresses confidentiality but does not mention data protection law or retention. | [NFR01](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR01), [NFR05](../requirements/02-non-functional-requirements.md#NFR05), [FR14](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR14) | Data is kept too long or deleted too soon. | [Q20](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q20) |
| <a id="A18"></a>A18 | Funder reports give totals of requests, completed journeys, cancellations by reason and accessible-vehicle journeys for a date range, with any count below 5 shown as "<5". | The scenario requires summary reports but not their content. In small rural communities, small counts could identify individuals. | [FR13](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR13) | Reports do not meet funders' needs, or still allow passengers to be identified. | [Q21](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q21) |
| <a id="A19"></a>A19 | Referral emails are not stored in the system; staff enter only the details needed for the journey. | The scenario does not say how referral emails are kept. | [FR14](../requirements/01-functional-requirements.md#FR14) | Clinical data remains in stored attachments that cannot be cleaned. | [Q22](04-stakeholder-questions.md#Q22) |
